Frequently asked questions

Is GIF Sticker Maker & GIF Editor free?

Yes. All tools are free: sticker packs, GIF stickers, AI stickers, status saver and wallpapers. The app is supported by ads.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer. It is available worldwide on Google Play.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app is for Android only for now.

Which languages does the app support?

The app adapts to your phone language, with full support for Arabic, English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, French, German, Italian, Russian, Hindi, Persian and Urdu, and English for other languages.

How do I make my first sticker?

Open the app, tap + and choose photo, video, GIF, text or AI. Save it, add at least 3 stickers to the pack, then tap Add to WhatsApp.

How long can an animated sticker be?

Between 0.5 and 8 seconds. Pick the best part of your video or GIF and the app trims it for you.

How many stickers does a pack need?

WhatsApp requires at least 3 stickers per pack and allows up to 30.

Can I mix photos and videos in one pack?

No. WhatsApp keeps animated and static stickers in separate packs, so the app does too.

Can I turn any GIF into a sticker?

Yes. Search millions of GIFs in the GIF tab, tap one and choose Make sticker, or edit it first.

How does the AI sticker maker work?

Type a short description, pick a style such as cartoon, 3D, emoji or anime, and the AI draws the sticker in seconds. You can also turn your own photo into an anime or cartoon sticker and make it move.

How do I remove the background from a photo?

Open the photo in the editor and tap Remove background. The AI cuts out the subject in one tap and leaves a transparent background.

Does the app support Arabic text?

Yes. It includes Arabic fonts such as Tajawal, Amiri, Reem Kufi and Lalezar, and right-to-left text works correctly.

Can I change the text outline color?

Yes. In text stickers, open the Outline tab and pick any color, or choose no outline at all.

How do I save WhatsApp statuses?

Watch the status in WhatsApp first, then open Status saver in the app and tap the download button. You can save it, share it or turn it into a sticker.

Can I set a GIF as my wallpaper?

Yes. Tap Set as wallpaper on any GIF or sticker to make it a live wallpaper, and add the GIF widget to your home screen.

Does it work with Telegram?

Yes. Every pack has an Add to Telegram button as well as Add to WhatsApp.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Packs are added through WhatsApp's own sticker system, so they appear in WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.

Can I share my stickers as a video for Status or Stories?

Yes. Export any sticker as a 9:16 video for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Stories, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and more.

Why doesn't my pack appear in WhatsApp?

Make sure WhatsApp is up to date and the pack has at least 3 stickers, then tap Add to WhatsApp again. Restarting WhatsApp usually shows the new pack.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Stickers are made on your phone. Only the AI features send your text or photo to generate the image. See the privacy policy for details.

Is GIF Sticker Maker & GIF Editor a GIF keyboard?

No. It is a sticker maker. Instead of searching GIFs in a keyboard, you create your own GIF stickers and add them to WhatsApp, where they appear in your sticker tray.

What are WAStickerApps?

WAStickerApps is the name WhatsApp uses for apps that add sticker packs to WhatsApp. GIF Sticker Maker & GIF Editor is one of them: tap Add to WhatsApp and your pack is installed.

Can I make romantic or Islamic GIF stickers?

Yes. Use your own photos or videos, add text in Arabic or any language, and save them as a love, romantic or Islamic sticker pack.

What does GIF stand for?

Graphics Interchange Format, a short looping animation that works great as a sticker.

How can I contact support?

Use the Contact page or the Help button inside the app. We read every message.
