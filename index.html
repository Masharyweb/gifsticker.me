A stickers app made for WhatsApp

Most sticker apps give you ready-made packs. GIF Sticker Maker works the other way: it is a sticker maker and a simple GIF maker in one. Pick any moment from a video, trim it into a short loop, and it becomes an animated sticker you can send in WhatsApp chats, just like stickers for text messages.

Your stickers whatsapp packs are saved on your phone, so you can add, edit or delete them any time. The app also includes a GIF library, so you can turn trending GIFs into GIF stickers without leaving the app.

Questions

Is GIF Sticker Maker free?

Yes. You can make sticker packs and add them to WhatsApp for free.

How do I turn a GIF into a WhatsApp sticker?

Open the GIF library in the app, pick a GIF, trim it if needed and tap Add to WhatsApp.

How long can an animated sticker be?

Between 0.5 and 8 seconds.

Is GIF Sticker Maker a GIF keyboard?

No. It is a sticker maker. Instead of searching GIFs in a keyboard, you create your own GIF stickers and add them to WhatsApp, where they appear in your sticker tray.

What are WAStickerApps?

WAStickerApps is the name WhatsApp uses for apps that add sticker packs to WhatsApp. GIF Sticker Maker is one of them: tap Add to WhatsApp and your pack is installed.

Can I make romantic or Islamic GIF stickers?

Yes. Use your own photos or videos, add text in Arabic or any language, and save them as a love, romantic or Islamic sticker pack.
