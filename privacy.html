Privacy Policy

GIF Sticker Maker & GIF Editor · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the app GIF Sticker Maker & GIF Editor (package com.whatsappstickers.stickermakerforwhatsapp) does with your information.

1. Your photos, videos and GIFs

The app processes the photos, videos and GIFs you choose on your device to create stickers. We do not upload them to our servers.

2. WhatsApp statuses

If you grant access, the app reads the statuses (photos and videos) that are on your device so you can save them or turn them into stickers. This happens only on your device. We do not upload these files.

3. Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

4. AI sticker generation

When you use the AI sticker feature, the text you type is sent to our AI processing service (Cloudflare Workers AI) to create the image. The text is encrypted in transit and is not stored by us after the image is created.

5. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network and Unity Ads. These networks may collect and use your advertising ID and device information to show ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. You can also see the ad consent options inside the app.

6. Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. This data is anonymous and does not include your photos or the content you create.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, and only for the purposes described.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

You can remove the app at any time, which deletes the local files it created. To request deletion of any data we hold, contact us at mashary11112@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always published on this page.

11. Contact

Email: mashary11112@gmail.com
