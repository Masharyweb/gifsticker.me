Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed.
  • Your stickers are created and saved on your device.
  • Ads, analytics and crash reports are provided by Google services, which collect some device data.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Your photos and videos

The app only opens the photos, videos and GIFs you choose. They are processed on your device to create stickers. Finished stickers are stored on your device and are shared with WhatsApp only when you tap Add to WhatsApp. We do not upload your media to our servers.

Permissions

The app asks only for access needed to pick media from your device and to show ads. It does not request your contacts, microphone or precise location.

Data retention and deletion

Stickers stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Data collected by Google services is kept according to Google's retention settings. To ask about data linked to your device, email us.

Your rights and choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice.
  • You can ask us to access or delete information related to you by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data sent to third-party services is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
